<p> Hi! I'm Piyush Gandhi, a development economist and Researcher at Precision Development (PxD) (https://precisiondev.org). I work on agriculture, climate and the environment in low- and middle-income countries, using field experiments, satellite remote sensing and administrative data. </p>

<p> At PxD, I lead the evaluation of the World Bank's ACReSAL digital advisory program for smallholder farmers in Nigeria. In India, I work with the Ministry of Agriculture to use A/B-test evidence in scaling AI-powered monsoon forecasts to more than 50 million farmers.</p>

<p>My academic research studies how to reduce crop residue burning in India, the energy effects of micro-irrigation, and how giving workers a voice affects garment-factory workers' productivity and mental health. I received my PhD in Economics from UC Santa Cruz in 2026 and hold an MPhil in Economics from the University of Oxford. </p>

 <p>Feel free to reach out to me at <a href="mailto:pigandhi@ucsc.edu">pigandhi@ucsc.edu</a>.</p>
